Ways we could work together on

You mentioned people mostly open Snitch to unlock an app or answer a friend's request. It's a screen-time app, so you don't want them spending time in it anyway. Most of it happens in tiny moments, like checking the pot, asking a friend for 15 more minutes, or approving someone else's request. So I'd focus on those, and on how the app feels when you use it.

What I'd be curious about

One of your App Store reviews says Snitch is “just harsh enough to actually stop you using the apps, but not so harsh that you'll delete the app.” That balance is what I'd keep in mind for all of this.

Consistency

Does the app feel like one thing from screen to screen, and the same on iPhone and Android now that Android is out? And is the Figma file set up so your team can keep it that way?

Flows

Unlocking, begging the pot for more time, and approving a friend's request. People do it all the time, so it should take seconds.

Copy

Your website already sounds like friends talking. I'd check the app keeps that voice in the small moments, like the reason prompt or when someone eats the pot.

Paying

How free, solo premium and group premium come across, what happens when the 14-day trial ends, and how invite codes fit in.

Getting offline

How the hours you save turn into plans with friends, since that's what makes it feel rewarding and not just restrictive.
