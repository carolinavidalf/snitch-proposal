How it goes

A first project
  1. 1We pick the focus together on a short call.
  2. 2I work on it and share things as I go, so you're never waiting for one big reveal. I design in Figma and use AI tools like Claude Code to try ideas out quickly, and I keep my files tidy so your team can pick them up easily.
  3. 3We finish with a walkthrough where I explain everything and you can ask me anything.

I'd expect a small project to fit in a week or two and a full audit in about two, depending on how quickly I can get access and feedback.

What I'd look at

Consistency

How much the app feels like one thing from screen to screen, and how the Figma file is built so your team can keep it that way.

Flows

How easy it is to move through the moments that matter, with a close eye on the ones people go through most.

Copy

The words in the app, especially in the moments where a few well chosen lines can change how someone feels or what they do next.

Paying

What makes people feel good about paying, and where the app could make that easier or clearer.

This comes from what you told me on our call. I'll adjust it once I've spent proper time in the app.
