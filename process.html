How it goes

A first project
  1. 1We hop on a short call and pick the focus together.
  2. 2Then I get going and share bits as I work, so you're never left waiting for a big reveal. I design in Figma, play around with AI tools like Claude Code to try ideas out quickly, and keep my files tidy so your team can pick them up easily.
  3. 3We finish with a walkthrough where I explain everything and you can ask me whatever you want.

A small project usually fits in a week or two and a full audit in about two, depending on how fast I can get access and feedback.

What I'd be curious about

Consistency

Does the app feel like one thing from screen to screen, and is the Figma file set up so your team can keep it that way?

Flows

How easy it is to get through the moments that matter, especially the ones people go through all the time.

Copy

The words in the app, especially where a couple of well chosen lines can change how someone feels or what they do next.

Paying

What makes people feel good about paying, and where the app could make that easier or clearer.

This is based on what you told me on our call, and I'll change it once I've spent proper time in the app.
