How it goes

A first project
  1. 1We hop on a short call and pick the focus together.
  2. 2Then I get going and share bits as I work, so you see things as they come together. I design in Figma, build quick prototypes with tools like Claude Code to test ideas early, and keep my files tidy so your team can pick them up easily.
  3. 3We finish with a walkthrough where I explain everything and you can ask me whatever you want.

A small project is about 2 days of work, usually spread over a week or two so there's time for your feedback. A review of the core flows takes about two weeks. Both depend on how fast I can get access and feedback.

Next step
