Ways to work together

Start small
A small first project
£1,200 about 2 days

Pick the one thing that matters most to you right now and we go deep on it together. We choose it after I've spent proper time in the app, so it's based on what I actually see and on what you know about your users. You get the designs and a walkthrough call where I go through everything.

Go bigger
Full app audit
£1,800 about 3 days

I go through the whole app twice, once like a brand new user and once like someone who's been using it for a while, and I look at the copy and how consistent things feel from screen to screen. You get a list of what I'd fix first and why, mockups of the top changes, and a call where I walk you through it all.

Design system and Figma file
from £1,200

Tidying how the design is built so the app feels like one thing and your team can move faster, from components and styles to how the Figma file is organised. It's especially handy around a redesign. The size depends on what you already have, so I'd start by having a look at your current file.

A focused pack
from £1,200

For after a first project, when you want to keep going one topic at a time, like a flow, the UX copy, or what helps people decide to pay. We agree the scope together and it's priced in days.

Over time
By the hour or by the day
£90 per hour · £600 per day

For smaller things as they come up, whenever you'd rather just book some time. It can also be a regular thing, like one day a week where I take one feature at a time from a rough idea to something ready to build. We check in at the end of each month and can pause or change it at any point.

Prices are in pounds and a day means around 7 hours. If something doesn't fit the budget, tell me and we'll shrink the scope so it still works for a small team.
